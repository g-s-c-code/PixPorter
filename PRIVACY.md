# PixPorter Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

PixPorter is an image format converter for Windows, published by GSC.

## What PixPorter collects

Nothing. PixPorter does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

- **Your images stay on your device.** PixPorter reads the files you choose and writes the converted files to the folder you choose, entirely on your computer. No file, image content or metadata is ever uploaded.
- **No accounts.** PixPorter has no sign-in and no user accounts.
- **No analytics or tracking.** PixPorter contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash-reporting services, and makes no network connections.
- **Nothing is saved about you.** PixPorter only reads Windows' light/dark mode setting so it can match it.

## Microsoft Store

If you obtained PixPorter from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the purchase, download and updates under its own privacy statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement. The publisher receives only the aggregate sales and usage reports that Microsoft provides to all Store publishers.

## Children

PixPorter collects no data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new date.
